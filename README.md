## The entry

> CAUTION: Passengers from the future are asked not to correct the pilot. He is you.

My entry for the make.fun competition "Funniest Time Machine Warning Label". It is written as a flat, official safety notice for time travellers, and it stays in that deadpan voice right up to the last three words — when the pilot turns out to be the very passenger being warned.

## How it works

Running `python3 main.py` writes the entry text, exactly as shown above, to `/output/entry.txt` with no trailing newline.
